THE FOUNDATION

AI starts with data. Data annotation makes that data useful.

AI systems learn from data, but raw data does not always tell a machine what it means. Annotation adds the information needed to organize, describe, and interpret that data.

01

Raw Data

Start with images, text, audio, video, or geographic imagery collected for an AI task.

02

Data Annotation

Add labels, categories, boundaries, transcriptions, or attributes that give data meaning.

03

Quality Check

Review annotations for accuracy, consistency, completeness, and adherence to project guidelines.

04

Reliable Datasets

High-quality annotations create reliable datasets to train and evaluate performance-ready AI models.
