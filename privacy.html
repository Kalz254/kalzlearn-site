Your Privacy Matters

How we collect, use, and protect your information when using Kalz Learn and our tools.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We believe in collecting only the information necessary to provide you with helpful educational tools and resources. Depending on how you interact with Kalz Learn, we may collect the following types of information:

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

You may voluntarily provide personal information to us when you interact with our website, such as:

  • Contact Details: Your name and email address when you submit forms on our contact page.
  • Resource Requests: Your email address when you opt in or request digital resources or guides.
  • Purchases: Payment and billing details when purchasing workshops or digital products.

B. Assessment & Tool Data

The AI Learning Path assessment does not require an email address or account creation to complete.

When you use the AI Learning Path tool, the answers you provide are processed temporarily to determine your personalized recommendations and skill path. These responses are used solely to display your results in your browser during your active session and are not linked to your personal identity.

C. Technical & Analytics Data

We may collect limited, non-identifying technical information when you visit our website, including browser type, operating system, general location data, device type, and page usage patterns.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for specific, limited purposes, including:

  • Delivering requested resources, digital products, and workshop access.
  • Processing AI Learning Path assessment responses to show tailored results.
  • Responding to your contact requests, inquiries, and customer service needs.
  • Improving our educational tools, website functionality, and overall content performance.
  • Sending educational content, product updates, or announcements if you have subscribed to our communications.

3. Email Communications & Subscriptions

We strictly adhere to standard email privacy practices:

  • We do not require an email address to take or complete the AI Learning Path assessment.
  • If you explicitly request a resource, subscribe to updates, or purchase a product, your email address is used to deliver that content.
  • You may opt out or unsubscribe from marketing and promotional communications at any time using the Unsubscribe link provided at the bottom of every email.

4. Analytics, Cookies & Third-Party Services

To keep Kalz Learn running reliably and efficiently, we work with trusted third-party service providers:

  • Analytics Tools: We use tools like Google Analytics to measure general traffic, visitor engagement, and popular site pathways.
  • Cookies & Local Technologies: Cookies and local storage technologies help remember preferences, support form functionality, and analyze visitor interactions. You can modify or disable cookies directly within your browser settings.
  • Operational Platforms: We utilize third-party platforms for digital product delivery, payment processing, email delivery, and form management (such as Payhip, MailerLite, or Google Sheets). These platforms manage data according to their own independent privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take standard technical and procedural safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, because no online transmission method is completely secure, we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information you have provided to us. You can submit data requests or ask questions about how your information is handled by reaching out via our contact page.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our website, tools, or legal requirements. Updated policies will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or how data is handled across our tools, please reach out through our contact page.
