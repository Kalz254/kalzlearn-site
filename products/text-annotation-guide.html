Why Text Data Requires Human Context

Human language is filled with ambiguity, slang, double meanings, and context-dependent entities. An automated parser often struggles to tell whether "Apple" refers to a fruit or a corporation without human annotation context.

This guide provides practical steps for identifying exact text spans, extracting entities like people, places, dates, and organizations, and categorizing customer intent and sentiment correctly.

What This Guide Helps You Understand

Gain a complete understanding of NLP data labeling workflows:

  • Understand the core mechanics of Natural Language Processing (NLP) data
  • Highlight exact text spans for Named Entity Recognition (NER)
  • Tag sentiment (positive, negative, neutral) and user intent accurately
  • Avoid incorrect entity boundaries and over/under-annotation errors
  • Handle slang, sarcasm, and ambiguous phrasing systematically
  • Apply document-level taxonomy and multi-label text categorization

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Named Entity Recognition (NER)?
NER is the process of locating and tagging key entities in text—such as names of people, places, organizations, dates, and amounts.

Does this cover prompt evaluation or LLM labeling?
This guide focuses directly on core NLP tasks like NER, sentiment analysis, intent classification, and text categorization.
